## 7. Method

1. `GET https://registry.quarkus.io/client/extensions/all`; for every non-core artifact the highest version was kept and its `metadata.extension-dependencies` (transitive extension dependencies computed at the extension's build time) checked for `io.quarkus:quarkus-security`, `io.quarkus:quarkus-spring-security`, `io.quarkus:quarkus-vertx-http`, `io.quarkus:quarkus-rest-jackson` / `quarkus-resteasy-reactive-jackson`. Every Quarkiverse artifact in the registry carries this metadata.
2. `GET /orgs/quarkiverse/repos` (GitHub API), `git clone --depth 1` of every repository, `grep -rnwE` for the symbols over every file, plus a scan of all `pom.xml`/Gradle files for direct dependencies on the affected artifacts.
3. `GET /search/code` (GitHub API) per symbol for `org:quarkiverse`, and for the non-Quarkiverse repositories of section 5. The index was sanity-checked with `HttpAuthenticationMechanism` (8 results in `org:quarkiverse`).
